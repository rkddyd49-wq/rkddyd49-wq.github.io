Privacy Policy

Survive the Dinosaurs (공룡한테 살아남기)
Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Survive the Dinosaurs (the "Game"), how it is used and shared, and what choices you have. The Game is developed and operated by pkyappapk (피케이와이앱), a sole proprietorship represented by KangYong Park ("we", "us", "our"). If the Korean version and this English version differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We do not run a game server that stores your progress.
  • The Game shows rewarded video ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). Unity Ads collects device and advertising information to show and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or bank details.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Game data stored on your device

Your progress (level, items, gear, dinosaurs, camp layout, gold and Cash, quest and attendance records, mailbox, sound and language settings, ad-removal status) is saved in the app's storage on your device and is not sent to us.

2.2 Advertising data (Unity Ads)

When rewarded ads are prepared, requested or shown, the Unity Ads SDK and Unity's advertising partners may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate location derived from it (country or region level)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, network type and app version
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or clicked and whether a rewarded ad was completed
  • Diagnostic data about ad performance and errors

This information is used to deliver ads, give you rewards for watched ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads based on your interests. Please also read the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

2.3 In-app purchase data (Google Play)

Purchases of Cash, the Starter Pack and "Remove Ads" are processed by Google Play Billing under Google's terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive your payment card or bank information. To deliver and restore your purchases, the Game receives purchase information from Google Play, such as the product ID, order ID and purchase token, and stores it on your device.

2.4 Customer support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any information you choose to include (such as your device model or order number).

3. How we use information

  • To run the Game and keep your progress on your device
  • To deliver items you purchase and to restore "Remove Ads"
  • To show ads and grant rewards for rewarded ads
  • To answer your questions and handle refunds or complaints
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to comply with legal obligations

4. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the Game or clearing the app's data in Android settings permanently deletes it.
  • Advertising data is retained by Unity and its partners according to their own policies.
  • Support emails and records of complaints or disputes are kept for 3 years, as required by the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and then deleted.

5. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal information. Information is processed by the following companies, partly outside the Republic of Korea (including the United States and the European Union):

Unity Technologies Inc. and its advertising partners

  • Country: United States and others
  • Data: advertising data described in 2.2
  • Purpose: delivering and measuring ads, fraud prevention
  • Retention: according to Unity's privacy policy
  • When and how: sent encrypted over the network when ads are prepared or requested

Google LLC

  • Country: United States and others
  • Data: purchase data described in 2.3
  • Purpose: payment processing and purchase verification
  • Retention: according to Google's privacy policy
  • When and how: sent through Google Play when you pay

We may also disclose information to public authorities when required by law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads. The exact menu depends on your device.
  • Access, correction, deletion and suspension: email rkddyd49@gmail.com and we will respond within 10 days. Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can also delete it yourself by uninstalling the Game.
  • Advertising data: you can make privacy requests about data collected by Unity through https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. California residents can use https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information.
  • Residents of the EU, the UK and other regions may have additional rights under local law, including the right to object, to restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and the advertising and payment SDKs send data over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

9. Changes to this policy

We will announce changes in the Game at least 7 days before they take effect (30 days for important or unfavorable changes). The effective date at the top shows when the policy was last updated.
