Privacy Policy

fishcakeTycoon (Fishcake Tycoon)
Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play fishcakeTycoon (the "Game"), how it is used and shared, and what choices you have. The Game is developed and operated by pkyappapk (피케이와이앱), a sole proprietorship represented by KangYong Park ("we", "us", "our").

1. Summary

  • The Game does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device. We do not run a game server that stores your progress.
  • The Game shows advertisements through Unity LevelPlay. LevelPlay and its advertising partners collect device and advertising information to show and measure ads.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your card or bank details.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Game data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (restaurant layout, staff, virtual currency, settings such as sound and graphics, offline-reward time, coupons used and mailbox items) in files on your device. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

2.2 Advertising data (Unity LevelPlay)

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies, formerly ironSource) to show banner ads and rewarded video ads. When ads are requested or shown, LevelPlay and the advertising networks it works with may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, network type and app version
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, viewed or clicked, and whether a rewarded ad was completed
  • Diagnostic data about ad performance and errors

This information is used to deliver ads, give you rewards for watched rewarded ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted, show ads based on your interests. According to Unity, data collected by the LevelPlay SDK is encrypted in transit. Please also read the Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

2.3 In-app purchase data (Google Play)

Purchases of gold coins and "Remove Ads" are processed by Google Play Billing under Google's terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive your payment card or bank information. To deliver and restore your purchases, the Game receives purchase information from Google Play, such as the product ID, order ID and purchase token. This information is stored on your device.

2.4 Customer support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message and any information you choose to include. The support email template in the Game adds the app version, device model and operating system version so that we can solve your problem; you can delete them before sending.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Game and keep your progress on your device
  • To deliver items you purchase and to restore "Remove Ads"
  • To show ads and grant rewards for rewarded ads
  • To answer your questions and handle refunds or complaints
  • To prevent fraud and abuse and to comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only as described below:

  • Unity Technologies (LevelPlay) and its advertising network partners, for advertising as described in Section 2.2
  • Google, for payment processing and purchase verification through Google Play
  • Public authorities, when required by law

Some of these companies may process information outside the Republic of Korea, including in the United States and the European Union, under their own privacy policies and appropriate safeguards.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device is kept until you delete it. Uninstalling the Game or clearing the app's data in Android settings permanently deletes it.
  • Advertising data is retained by Unity and its partners according to their own policies.
  • Support emails and records of complaints or disputes are kept for up to 3 years, as required by the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, and then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads: on Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in Settings > Privacy > Ads (the exact menu depends on your device).
  • Remove Ads: the "Remove Ads" purchase removes banner ads and lets you receive rewarded bonuses without watching ads.
  • Access, correction and deletion: you may ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it by emailing rkddyd49@gmail.com. We will respond within 10 days. Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can also delete it yourself by uninstalling the Game.
  • Advertising data: you can make privacy requests about data collected by Unity through https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. California residents can use https://unity.com/legal/do-not-sell-my-personal-information.
  • Residents of the EU, the UK and other regions may have additional rights under local law, including the right to object, to restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and the advertising and payment SDKs send data over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. If we make important changes, we will notify you in the Game (for example, through Notices) at least 7 days before they take effect. The effective date at the top shows when the policy was last updated.
